A file manager that lives in your bar.

QuickFile opens as a real window docked to the edge of the screen. Your tiled windows move aside instead of hiding underneath, and Alt+Tab reaches it like any other app.

Smart search

Ask in plain English, Russian or Ukrainian: photos from last summer, the contract pdf, where is the invoice from march. An optional local model, Laya, runs on your CPU and offline. It sees the words you type — never file names, paths or contents.

Six exact modes stay one click away: fuzzy, contains, exact, prefix, suffix and regex. Without the model, Smart still ranks your keywords.

Offline drives New

Index a USB drive once. Search it after it's back in the drawer, and QuickFile tells you which drive to plug in.

Safe by default

Copy, move, rename and duplicate with an explicit choice on every conflict. Deleting goes to Trash, and Ctrl+Z takes it back.

Preview on Space

Text, images and folders preview right in the sidebar. Shift+Space hands off to Sushi.

Devices

USB sticks appear on their own. One click mounts and opens a drive; one button ejects it safely.

Git context

Branch and per-file status in the tree, plus a Git tab for whatever you selected.

Project knowledge

See the rules your coding agents read — Claude, Codex, Gemini, Cursor, Copilot, Windsurf — with an estimate of what they cost in tokens.

Live, not polled

Native filesystem events update rows in place. Your selection, scroll position and unsaved notes stay where they were.

Theme-native

Colors, type and spacing come from your Omarchy theme. Switch to Osaka Jade, and so does QuickFile.

Find files on drives that aren't plugged in.

  1. 01

    Index

    Press next to a drive in DEVICES. QuickFile walks it once into a private catalog: names, paths, sizes and dates. Never contents.

  2. 02

    Unplug

    The drive stays listed, dimmed: Not connected · indexed 3d ago. Plug it back in, and it re-indexes in the background.

  3. 03

    Search anyway

    Its files show up after your live results, marked OFFLINE. Press Enter and QuickFile names the drive to connect. Plug it in, and the row comes alive where it is.

Local. Offline. Yours.

logsecurity model
  • [ OK ]No telemetry, no accounts, no cloud. Nothing about your files leaves the machine.
  • [ OK ]The Smart Search model runs offline on the CPU. It sees your query — never file names, paths or contents.
  • [ OK ]The only network request is the model download, and only after you confirm it.
  • [ OK ]No root and no system changes. Notes, history and drive catalogs live in private 0600 files under your home.
  • [ OK ]Filenames are treated as untrusted input: shown as plain text, passed as arguments, never through a shell.
  • [ OK ]Delete means Trash. Deleting for good needs its own confirmation.

$ omarchy plugin add https://github.com/baranskyi/omarchy-quickfile.git --enable

For the shortcut, add one line to ~/.config/hypr/bindings.lua:

o.bind("SUPER + B", "QuickFile sidebar", "omarchy-shell shell toggle m0sthatedman.quickfile")
